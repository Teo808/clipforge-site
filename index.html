Clipforge

Clipforge is a desktop app that helps creators turn longer videos into short vertical clips for review, export, and publishing.

Clipforge is designed to simplify the short form content workflow by helping users create clips from longer videos, organize them, review finished exports, and publish supported content to connected social media platforms.

What Clipforge does

Creates short vertical clips from longer video content.

Lets users review and manage rendered clips.

Helps users publish clips to supported platforms through connected accounts.

Who it is for

Clipforge is built for creators, editors, and users who want an easier way to prepare and publish short form video content.
