Privacy Policy

ClipForge is a desktop video editing app. Users choose videos to process and choose whether to upload them to connected platforms.

Authentication tokens, settings, and generated files may be stored locally by the app on the user’s computer unless otherwise stated.

ClipForge does not sell personal data.

If platform integrations such as TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram are used, those platforms may process uploaded content and account information according to their own policies.
