Privacy Policy for Clipforge

Effective date: April 17, 2026

Clipforge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Clipforge handles information when you use the application.

Information Clipforge may process

Clipforge may process video files selected or created by the user.

Clipforge may process clip metadata and export information.

Clipforge may process account connection details and access tokens for supported social media platforms.

Clipforge may process basic app settings and preferences saved locally for app functionality.

How Clipforge uses information

Clipforge uses this information only to create and manage video clips.

Clipforge uses this information to save user preferences and project settings.

Clipforge uses this information to connect to supported platforms when the user chooses to publish content.

Clipforge uses this information to improve app functionality and reliability.

Local processing

Clipforge is a desktop application. Video processing and file handling may occur locally on the user’s device.

Third party services

If a user connects Clipforge to third party platforms such as social media services, those platforms may receive information required for publishing or account authentication. Use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data sharing

Clipforge does not sell user data. Information is only used as necessary to operate the application and connected publishing features chosen by the user.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for the content they create, upload, process, and publish through Clipforge.

Security

Clipforge uses reasonable efforts to protect settings and connected account information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Clipforge after updates means the user accepts the revised policy.

Contact
